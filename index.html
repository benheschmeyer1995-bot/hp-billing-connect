HP Billing

Heschmeyer Pools' own billing tool

HP Billing turns the month's pool service records into QuickBooks Online invoices for Heschmeyer Pools' customers. It is used only by Heschmeyer Pools staff, and it is not sold or offered to anyone else.

It runs on the office computer, not on this website. To open it, start HP Billing on that computer, then:

Open HP Billing

Connecting to QuickBooks

In HP Billing, go to Setup → QuickBooks → Connect to QuickBooks and approve the connection in QuickBooks. HP Billing then reads the customer and item lists and creates the invoices you approve, one at a time.
