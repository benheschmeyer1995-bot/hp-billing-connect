HP Billing is disconnected from QuickBooks

HP Billing can no longer read from or create anything in this QuickBooks company.

Invoices it already created stay in QuickBooks as they are. HP Billing keeps its own record of them too.

What next

  • In HP Billing, open Setup → QuickBooks and press Disconnect as well, so it stops trying to use the old connection.
  • To connect again, press Connect to QuickBooks there and approve it.
