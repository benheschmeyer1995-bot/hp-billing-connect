Privacy policy

HP Billing, Heschmeyer Pools' internal billing tool · effective September 14, 2026

Who this covers

HP Billing is used only by Heschmeyer Pools to invoice its own pool-service customers through its own QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What HP Billing reads and writes in QuickBooks

  • Reads the company name, the customer list (names, email addresses and billing addresses) and the product and service list, so each invoice goes to the right customer with the right items.
  • Creates invoices that Heschmeyer Pools staff have reviewed and approved, and asks QuickBooks to email them to the customer when told to.
  • Reads back invoices it created, to confirm they exist and whether they were emailed.

It does not read bank, payroll, payment or tax information.

Where the information is kept

HP Billing runs on Heschmeyer Pools' own computer. Everything it keeps is stored in a file on that computer: the service records it bills from, the customer and item matches, a record of each invoice it created, and the key QuickBooks issues for the connection. None of it is sent to any server run by HP Billing, because there is none.

The information goes only to QuickBooks, over an encrypted connection. It is never sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

This website

These pages exist because QuickBooks requires secure web addresses for connecting and disconnecting. They have no forms, cookies, analytics or trackers. The connection page passes QuickBooks' one-time answer straight to HP Billing on the same computer and keeps nothing. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web-server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Disconnecting and deleting

The QuickBooks connection can be ended at any time, from HP Billing's Setup screen or from QuickBooks' own list of connected apps. Doing so stops all access straight away. Information HP Billing kept is deleted by deleting its data file from the office computer.
